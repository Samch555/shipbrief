# ShipBrief review log (fill in by hand, about 2 minutes a day)

How to use it: copy the "Day template" block for each day you read the brief and fill it in right after reading.
Keep answers short. `scripts/quality_report.py` counts a day as filled once **Read it?** has an answer.
After 14 days, run `python scripts/quality_report.py` and read `REVIEW_REPORT.md` together with this log.

Scales: 1 = poor, 5 = excellent.
